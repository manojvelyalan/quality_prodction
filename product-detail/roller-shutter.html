ROLLER SHUTTER

Heavy-Duty Extruded Security & Thermal Insulation Shutters

Architectural System: ROLLER SHUTTER installed by Quality Space LLC. Precision European Engineering
System Overview

Designed for Architectural Distinction & Performance

Engineered for superior burglary resistance, storm protection, and acoustic insulation, Quality Space Roller Shutters are manufactured from double-walled extruded or polyurethane foam-insulated aluminum profiles. When rolled down, they form an impenetrable thermal barrier that reflects harsh solar heat in summer, locks in indoor climate efficiency, and creates complete darkness for master suites and home cinemas.

Key Engineering Highlights

  • Certified RC2 / RC3 forced-entry security resistance with automated anti-lift lock springs
  • Dense polyurethane foam core provides thermal insulation and reduces ambient outside noise by up to 10 dB
  • 100% room darkening capability ideal for bedrooms, home theaters, and luxury hotel suites
  • Compact roll diameter fits seamlessly into recessed ceiling lintels or architectural exterior boxes
  • Smart obstacle detection prevents curtain damage if patio furniture or objects are below
