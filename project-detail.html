1. The Architectural Brief & Challenge

Set within the premier enclave of Emirates Hills, this bespoke 18,000 sq.ft. contemporary villa was designed with an ultra-minimalist aesthetic. The focal point of the residence is an expansive 16-meter wide, double-height living pavilion framed by slim-profile structural sliding glass doors rising to a vertical height of 5.8 meters.

The client and European architectural team sought to create an unbroken connection between the indoor travertine flooring and the exterior outdoor lounge and infinity pool deck. However, two critical climatic challenges emerged during the design phase:

  • Extreme Solar Heat Gain: Direct afternoon solar radiation would generate severe greenhouse heat through the vast glass envelope, driving up cooling requirements and causing unbearable glare.
  • Insects at Sunset: Opening the massive sliding panels during cooler autumn and winter evenings allowed desert mosquitoes and pests into the residence.
  • Zero Tolerance for Bulky Hardware: Conventional insect screens are structurally capped at 3 meters and require bulky intermediate mullions or heavy surface-mounted track lips, which would ruin the 16-meter uninterrupted architectural horizon.

2. The Integrated Quality Space Engineering Solution

Quality Space was engaged during the structural concrete stage to coordinate both the custom 5.8m oversized insect screening and external motorized solar shading into a cohesive, concealed envelope detail.

Our solution combined two complementary German-engineered technologies:

  • Quality Space 5.8M Continuous Insect Screens: Engineered with a proprietary internal counter-balancing cartridge and high-tenacity micro-weave mesh, allowing continuous single-span operation across 5.8m vertical drop without cloth sag or flutter.
  • WAREMA External Venetian Blinds: Fitted with 80mm rolled edge aluminum slats featuring marine-grade corrosion coatings. Positioned outside the glass, they intercept up to 88% of solar thermal radiation before it can touch the glass surface.
  • Recessed Concealed Head Pockets: Both the screen spools and Venetian blind head boxes were integrated into custom cast-in-place concrete ceiling recesses, rendering them 100% invisible from both inside and outside when raised.
  • Zero-Threshold Flush Floor Channels: The bottom guide rail is recessed totally flush with the finished Italian travertine tiles, incorporating sub-floor drainage weep slots for rain and terrace washdown.
"Quality Space achieved what three other international contractors told us was technically impossible: screening a 5.8-meter vertical opening without intermediate transoms or visible floor lips." - Project Lead Architect, Contemporary European Design Practice
